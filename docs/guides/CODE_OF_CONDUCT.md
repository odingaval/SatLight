# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want Satlight to be welcoming to everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, location, or language.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, patient, and constructive in reviews and discussions.
- Give credit, assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or doxxing
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention
- Spam, bounty farming, collusion, or deliberate manipulation of payouts
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, forfeit bounties, or ban accounts. Report issues to **conduct@<your-domain>**. Reports are handled confidentially.
